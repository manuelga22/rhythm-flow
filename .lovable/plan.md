# iPhone App Redesign

## Goal
Transform the current desktop-oriented mock-up into a polished, mobile-first experience that feels native to an iPhone while keeping the full practice journey intact.

## What will change
- Replace the desktop header with a compact iOS-style top bar and persistent bottom navigation.
- Recompose every step for a single-column iPhone screen with comfortable thumb reach and safe-area spacing.
- Make recording the visual center: large circular record control, full-width waveform, clear timer, and minimal supporting actions.
- Present feedback as vertically stacked score summaries, swipe-friendly comparison sections, and three concise coaching actions.
- Refine source selection, improvisation, and session completion into focused full-screen mobile states.
- Preserve the selected editorial identity through bold condensed headings, warm paper tones, orange actions, green success states, and restrained motion.

## Interaction details
- Keep the existing simulated recording, playback, retry, and step progression.
- Use icon-led controls with accessible labels and generous touch targets.
- Keep progress visible without crowding the screen.
- Ensure the layout works from small iPhones through larger phones without horizontal overflow.

## Validation
- Test the entire flow at a 390 × 844 iPhone viewport.
- Check key states visually and confirm all actions still advance correctly.
