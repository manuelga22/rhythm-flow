# Synchronized clip playback

## Goal
Make the full reference clip playable at the top of the Structure page and visually follow its phrases as it plays.

## Changes
- Add a prominent full-clip play/pause control above the structure summary.
- Show current time, total time, and an accessible playback progress bar.
- Advance mocked playback across the 31-second clip and stop/reset at the end.
- Highlight the current phrase in both the structure transcript and phrase list.
- Scroll the active phrase into view when needed, without disrupting manual browsing.
- Keep individual phrase playback controls; starting one playback mode stops the other.

## Validation
- Verify play, pause, resume, completion, progress, and synchronized highlighting.
- Check mobile and desktop layouts for overflow and browser errors.
