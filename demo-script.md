# Cadence: demo script (about 2:30)

About 330 spoken words, which fits 2:30 at a relaxed pace. **[Brackets]** are what's on screen. Everything else is what you say.

---

## 0:00–0:20 · The problem

**[Dashboard / landing page]**

> A lot of people speak English fluently. Their grammar is solid and they pronounce every word correctly, but they still don't *sound* native. That usually isn't about the words. It's about the music: which words get the beat, which ones get squeezed, where you pause, and where your pitch falls.
>
> Cadence is a studio for practicing exactly that. It trains rhythm and intonation, not pronunciation.

## 0:20–0:40 · The method

**[Open the Method page, scroll through the three steps]**

> The method has three steps. First, you pick a voice you want to sound like. Second, you shadow it and match its rhythm. Third, you improvise: the script disappears, and you say the same idea in your own words. That last step shows whether the rhythm actually carries over into real, spontaneous speech.

## Recording tips

- **Pre-process the clip before you record.** YouTube fetching and AI generation take a while. Record the waiting screens separately and trim them, or use a clip you already saved and open it with "Continue".
- **Pick a take that has flaws.** Feedback on a perfect take is boring. One obvious over-stressed word makes the demo land.
- **Use a short phrase** of about 5–10 seconds so the shadowing part stays tight.
